❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Does Handle Market transfer WhatsApp usernames?
No. Handle Market is a discovery, contact and negotiation marketplace. It does not control or guarantee a WhatsApp username transfer.
What is ⭐ Premium?
Premium is awarded by Handle Market to selected usernames and cannot be purchased by sellers.
What are 💎 VIP, 🔥 HOT and ⬆️ Bump?
They are promotion options that increase listing visibility. They are advertising, not verification.
I unlocked a contact but there is a problem. What should I do?
Create a support ticket, choose Contact Problem, and include the LOT number so the support team can review the listing.
